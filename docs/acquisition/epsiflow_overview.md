# EpsiFlow Overview

## 1. What EpsiFlow Does

EpsiFlow helps Shopify app developers and other eligible digital businesses pay for advertising and related online business expenses when their local banking or card infrastructure makes those payments difficult, unreliable, or impossible.

The core use case today is **Shopify app developers, particularly in India, who want to run Shopify Ads but face payment-method restrictions or unreliable international card acceptance**.

EpsiFlow gives these clients access to the payment infrastructure they need to run their advertising without having to solve the banking, foreign-card, or cross-border payment problem themselves.

Once a client is onboarded, EpsiFlow provides:

- A dedicated account through the EpsiFund application.
- A bank account created for the client.
- A digital debit card that can be used for eligible advertising spend.
- Visibility into advertising transactions and spending.
- Access to invoices through the EpsiFund account.
- Direct communication and support from the EpsiFlow team.
- Optional access to the EpsiFlow Slack community.

In practical terms, EpsiFlow sits between the client's growth ambition and the payment infrastructure required to execute it.

## 2. Core Customer Segment

The primary acquisition focus is currently on **Shopify app developers and Shopify SaaS businesses in India**.

The strongest-fit prospects are companies that:

- Have a Shopify app or a portfolio of Shopify apps.
- Want to acquire more merchants and grow app installs.
- Are considering, testing, or already using Shopify Ads.
- Have difficulty paying for Shopify Ads from India because of card or payment-method restrictions.
- Experience failed, rejected, or unreliable international advertising payments.
- Are losing growth opportunities because they cannot reliably fund their advertising.
- Already spend on Shopify Ads and could benefit from a more reliable payment setup.
- Have enough traction to justify paid acquisition but may still be founder-led or operating with a relatively small team.

Likely decision-makers include founders, co-founders, CEOs, growth leads, marketing leads, and other people directly responsible for app growth or advertising spend.

---

## 3. The Problem EpsiFlow Solves

For many Shopify app developers in India, the problem is not necessarily demand or willingness to advertise. The problem is often the infrastructure required to pay for the advertising in the first place.

A developer may know that Shopify Ads could help generate more app installs and merchant discovery, but still be unable to run campaigns consistently because the available payment methods are rejected, unsupported, inconvenient, or unreliable for the required international transactions.

This creates a gap between **wanting to grow** and **being able to pay for growth**.

EpsiFlow exists to remove that gap.

---

## 4. Customer Pains We Target

### 4.1 Inability to Pay for Shopify Ads

Some Indian Shopify developers cannot use their existing cards or banking setup to pay for Shopify Ads reliably. Even when they are willing to spend on acquisition, payment infrastructure becomes the blocker.

### 4.2 Rejected or Failed Advertising Payments

Even when a card appears suitable, international advertising transactions may fail or be rejected. This creates uncertainty around whether campaigns can continue running.

### 4.3 Advertising Campaign Interruptions

When a payment fails or the available balance becomes too low, advertising spend is rejected and campaigns can stop. This directly interrupts customer acquisition.

### 4.4 Lost Growth and Revenue Opportunity

Every period during which a developer cannot run ads is potentially a period in which competitors continue acquiring merchants while the developer does not.

The cost of the payment problem is therefore not just operational inconvenience. It can also represent:

- Lost app installs.
- Lost merchant acquisition.
- Slower growth.
- Delayed testing and learning from paid acquisition.
- Lost revenue that could have been generated from newly acquired users.

### 4.5 Banking and Forex Complexity

Developers may need to investigate international cards, prepaid forex products, banking restrictions, transaction limits, or alternative financial services simply to pay an advertising platform.

This is operational work that has very little to do with actually building or growing a Shopify app.

### 4.6 Lack of Reliability

Even if a developer finds a temporary payment workaround, reliability matters. A growth channel is difficult to scale if the founder cannot be confident that the payment method will continue working.

### 4.7 Administrative Friction

Managing advertising spend, payment records, and invoices across different systems creates unnecessary overhead. EpsiFlow centralizes visibility into eligible spending and invoices through the EpsiFund account.

## 5. Value Provided by EpsiFlow

### 5.1 Access to Working Payment Infrastructure

The most important value EpsiFlow provides is straightforward: **the ability to make advertising payments that the client may otherwise struggle to make through their existing banking setup**.

### 5.2 Ability to Run and Scale Shopify Ads

EpsiFlow enables developers to move from "we want to advertise" to "we can actually fund the campaign."

This removes a major operational blocker from paid acquisition.

### 5.3 Reduced Risk of Campaign Disruption

A dedicated payment setup helps clients avoid relying on cards or payment methods that frequently fail for the relevant transactions.

Clients can also monitor their available balance so they can fund the account before insufficient funds begin interrupting advertising spend.

### 5.4 Simpler Financial Operations

Clients can use their EpsiFund account to monitor:

- Advertising spending.
- Relevant transactions.
- Account activity.
- Invoices.

This creates a clearer operational view of the money being used for advertising.

### 5.5 Faster Path to Paid Acquisition

Instead of researching banks, card products, foreign-payment restrictions, and alternative payment providers, the client can use an infrastructure already designed around this use case.

### 5.6 Human Support

The onboarding process includes direct interaction with the EpsiFlow team, including a call to securely transfer the required card details and assistance when operational issues arise.

### 5.7 Community Access

Clients can optionally join the EpsiFlow Slack community. This creates an additional relationship layer beyond the payment service itself and gives EpsiFlow a direct communication channel with its customer base.

### 5.8 Broader Growth Support

EpsiFlow can also create additional value around the payment infrastructure itself. For example, EpsiFlow has explored partnerships related to Shopify Ads optimization so that clients are not only able to pay for advertising, but can also improve how efficiently that advertising is run.

The long-term value proposition can therefore evolve from simply **enabling advertising spend** toward **helping Shopify developers deploy growth capital more effectively**.

## 6. EpsiFlow's Positioning

A simple way to describe EpsiFlow is:

> **EpsiFlow gives Shopify app developers the payment infrastructure they need to run Shopify Ads when their existing banking setup cannot reliably support them.**

A more outcome-oriented positioning is:

> **EpsiFlow removes the payment barrier between Shopify app developers and paid customer acquisition.**

The distinction is important. The product is not merely a digital card. The value comes from enabling a business activity that otherwise cannot happen reliably.

The card, bank account, account dashboard, invoices, support, and community are the infrastructure through which that outcome is delivered.

## 7. Acquisition Goals

The current acquisition objective is to systematically identify and convert more Shopify app developers who experience the payment problem EpsiFlow solves.

### Primary Acquisition Goal

Build a predictable acquisition engine for **Indian Shopify app developers that have a real or near-term need for Shopify Ads payment infrastructure**.

### Supporting Goals

1. **Increase the number of qualified EpsiFlow clients.**
   The focus should be on customers who are likely to actively use the payment infrastructure rather than maximizing lead volume for its own sake.

2. **Identify prospects close to the moment of need.**
   The best prospect is not simply a Shopify developer. It is a Shopify developer who is actively growing, considering paid acquisition, launching something, discussing advertising, or experiencing a payment problem.

3. **Reduce customer acquisition cost through targeted outbound.**
   Current acquisition work is focused heavily on structured cold outreach using tools such as Apollo for lead sourcing and Instantly for email automation.

4. **Build a repeatable Ideal Customer Profile.**
   Lead generation should progressively identify which company sizes, app categories, growth stages, job titles, technologies, and behavioral signals correlate with successful EpsiFlow customers.

5. **Improve conversion from outreach to active spend.**
   A signed-up account alone is not the final goal. The economically meaningful milestone is a client who completes onboarding, funds the account, receives the payment setup, and begins using EpsiFlow for eligible advertising spend.

6. **Reduce the perceived friction of getting started.**
   The service itself is relatively straightforward once a client understands it. Acquisition messaging should therefore make the next step feel small and concrete rather than forcing the prospect through a heavy sales process.

7. **Develop referral and community-driven acquisition over time.**
   Because Shopify developers operate in a relatively connected ecosystem, satisfied clients, partnerships, Slack relationships, consultants, agencies, and other Shopify ecosystem participants may become increasingly important acquisition channels.

## 8. Current Acquisition Approach

The current outbound strategy focuses on finding relevant Shopify developers and contacting the people most likely to own the growth or payment problem.

The acquisition workflow being developed includes:

1. Identify Shopify app companies, primarily in India.
2. Identify the relevant founder, executive, growth, or marketing contact.
3. Filter prospects using company characteristics and behavioral signals.
4. Prioritize companies showing signs that they are actively growing or investing in acquisition.
5. Use cold email outreach to surface the Shopify Ads payment problem.
6. Explain the cost of leaving the problem unresolved, particularly lost advertising and growth opportunities.
7. Present EpsiFlow as the infrastructure already being used by other Shopify businesses to get the advertising payment setup working.
8. Use a low-friction call to action, such as offering to explain or share the setup used by existing clients.
9. Follow up with short emails that return to the original problem and CTA rather than introducing an entirely new sales pitch each time.
10. Convert interested prospects into the onboarding process.

Relevant signals can include activity around product launches, growth, Shopify development, advertising, partnerships, social activity, Reddit discussions, and other indicators that a company is actively trying to acquire customers or expand its app.

## 9. New Client Onboarding Pipeline

The current EpsiFlow onboarding flow is intentionally simple.

### Step 1: Client Confirms Interest

The prospect indicates that they want to proceed with EpsiFlow services.

At this stage, the sales conversation transitions into operational onboarding.

### Step 2: Client Creates an EpsiFund Account

The client creates an account in the EpsiFund application:

https://app.epsifund.com/

This account becomes the client's interface for managing and monitoring the service.

### Step 3: EpsiFlow Creates the Client's Bank Account

After registration, EpsiFlow creates a bank account for the client as part of the payment infrastructure.

### Step 4: EpsiFlow Generates a Digital Debit Card

A digital debit card is generated for the client. This card provides the payment method used for the relevant advertising spend.

### Step 5: Card Handover Call

EpsiFlow schedules a short call with the client to transfer the card details.

This provides a controlled handover and gives the team an opportunity to make sure the client understands how to use the setup.

### Step 6: Client Starts Using the Payment Infrastructure

The client can then use the provided card for the supported advertising payments, including the core Shopify Ads use case.

### Step 7: Spending and Invoice Tracking

Through the EpsiFund account, the client can monitor advertising spending and access invoices.

This gives the client ongoing visibility into account activity rather than treating the card as a standalone payment instrument.

### Step 8: Slack Community Invitation

EpsiFlow asks the client to provide the email address they use for Slack so that they can be invited to the EpsiFlow community.

Joining Slack is optional and is **not a hard onboarding requirement**.

The Slack community can be used for communication, support, updates, relationships, and additional value-added opportunities.

## 14. What EpsiFlow Is Not

For positioning purposes, it is useful to distinguish EpsiFlow from several adjacent categories.

EpsiFlow is not primarily:

- A generic consumer debit-card product.
- A traditional bank account offered for everyday banking.
- A Shopify Ads management agency.
- A general marketing agency.
- A lead-generation service.
- Merely a workaround card with no surrounding service.

The payment infrastructure is the mechanism. The business value is **unlocking reliable access to growth spend for customers who otherwise face financial infrastructure constraints**.

## 15. Strategic Direction

The immediate opportunity is narrow and concrete: solve the payment problem for Shopify developers who need to run Shopify Ads.

Over time, the larger opportunity is to become a broader financial and growth infrastructure layer for digital businesses that face similar cross-border payment constraints.

A logical progression is:

**Payment access**  
→ **Reliable advertising spend**  
→ **Spend visibility and financial operations**  
→ **Advertising optimization and partnerships**  
→ **Broader growth infrastructure for digital businesses**

Keeping the initial positioning narrow is useful for acquisition because the pain is easy to understand. The broader platform opportunity can develop after EpsiFlow owns a clearly defined problem exceptionally well.
